How it works

Employee Discipline: From facts to finished in four steps.

Set up once, then everyone's ready to go.

Trackument is configured once at the district level in just a few minutes. After that, every school site and department has instant, seamless access to a system custom-tailored to your district.

Whether the disciplinary document is being prepared by a principal, director, or assistant superintendent, Trackument automatically incorporates your district's applicable policies, bargaining agreement language, and legal requirements, from the California Education Code to the Vehicle Code, so administrators can create accurate, compliant documentation in minutes instead of hours.

Districtwide use of Trackument also ensures consistency of disciplinary documentation across the organization, minimizing claims arising from disparate treatment of employees.

Step 01

Describe what happened.

Enter the facts of the case in plain language. Note the employee's role, their classification, and the general category of the situation — attendance, performance, conduct, or something else. No legal language required; describe it the way you'd explain it to a colleague down the hall.

Step 02

Trackument finds the citations.

Every citation is checked against your board policies, bargaining agreements, Education Code, and vehicle and health codes as applicable. Citations are matched to the employee's actual classification and infraction, so a teacher and a bus driver never get the wrong policy language.

Step 03

Review and edit.

Read through the draft and adjust anything that needs your voice or your district's language. Trackument gives you a starting point already grounded in the right policies; you add the details only you would know, like tone, history, or context that shapes how the document should land.

Step 04

Download and deliver.

When the document is ready, you download it and it's yours to use.

At every step along the way you can edit your entries and change your selections. Nothing is locked in until you're ready to move forward.

Built for pre-Skelly documentation. Nothing past it.

Trackument is scoped to pre-Skelly documentation: conference summaries, written warnings, letters of reprimand, and notices of unsatisfactory performance. This is the work administrators handle themselves, day to day, without needing to bring in legal counsel. Trackument stops before dismissal proceedings begin, and its citations reflect that boundary.

In scope Conference summaries and written warnings
In scope Letters of reprimand
In scope Notices of unsatisfactory performance
Out of scope Dismissal statutes and Skelly proceedings
Out of scope Formal evaluation documents
