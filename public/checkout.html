Common questions

Do I need to pay for both a school site and a district license?

No. You sign up one way or the other, either as a district (covering every school and department under your ADA tier) or as an individual school site. You're never charged for both.

What counts as a "school site"?

This applies only if you're purchasing an individual site license rather than a district license. In that case, each school, continuation school, alternative education site, or district office location where administrators will use Trackument counts as one site. A district license already covers every school and department under your ADA tier, including the district office, at no extra charge.

How many administrators can use Trackument?

Every principal, assistant principal, and district administrator at your included sites can use Trackument at no additional cost.

What does custom training include?

Training is built around what your team actually needs. Common options include best practices for employee discipline documentation generally, hands-on training on using Trackument itself, onboarding sessions for new administrators, or a refresher for your team after a policy or bargaining agreement change. Check the box during signup and we'll follow up with you.

What if we need to add sites mid-year?

Contact us at sales@trackument.com and we'll send a prorated invoice for the additional sites.
