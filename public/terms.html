2. What Trackument Provides

Trackument is a web-based tool that guides site and district administrators through generating pre-discipline employee documentation, including conference summaries, written warnings, letters of reprimand, and notices of unsatisfactory performance. Trackument is scoped to pre-Skelly documentation only; it does not generate dismissal notices or documents related to formal Skelly proceedings.

Subject to this Agreement and payment of applicable fees, Trackument grants the District a non-exclusive, non-transferable, limited license to access and use Trackument for the District's internal HR and employee discipline documentation purposes, for the plan selected at signup (either a district-wide license covering all sites under the applicable ADA tier, or a single school site license).

The District will not sublicense, resell, or share access to Trackument with anyone outside its own administrators and staff without our prior written consent. The District retains all rights to the information it enters into Trackument. We retain all rights to the Trackument software itself, including its underlying code, templates, and system design.

4. Fees and Payment

Fees are based on the plan selected at signup, as shown on our Pricing page at the time of signup. Fees are billed annually and are due upon invoice, or charged at checkout if paying by card. Custom training, if requested, is quoted and billed separately.

5. Term, Renewal, and Termination

This Agreement begins on the date of signup and continues for one year. It renews automatically for successive one-year terms, and the payment method on file will be charged automatically at renewal, unless the District cancels before the renewal date. We will send a renewal reminder to the District's contact email at least 30 days before each renewal date, including instructions for how to cancel. Either party may otherwise terminate this Agreement for any reason with 60 days' written notice. Upon termination, the District may export any documents it generated through Trackument for 30 days, after which we may remove the District's access and associated data.

6. Data Privacy and Security

We maintain the confidentiality of all District data, including employee personnel information, consistent with California Education Code sections 49060–49085 and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), and we do not disclose District data except as required by law or with the District's prior written consent. Specifically:

  • All data transmitted between District users and Trackument is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Data at rest is encrypted through our hosting infrastructure, and credentials are stored as encrypted environment variables, never in application code.
  • Before any employee information reaches the underlying AI model, employee names and other identifying details are pseudonymized so the model never processes real identities.
  • Access to Trackument requires authenticated login credentials issued to the District's designated administrators.
  • We do not retain generated documents after a session ends, unless the District separately requests document storage.

We will notify the District in writing within 72 hours of discovering any unauthorized access to or disclosure of District data processed through Trackument.
