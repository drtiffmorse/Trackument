Who will manage your district's settings?

Settings managers can change your district information, board policies, bargaining agreements, document types, and handbooks. Everyone else at your district can use these settings but cannot change them. Add the people who will set up and maintain Trackument, such as your HR director. Each person you add gets an email explaining their role.
