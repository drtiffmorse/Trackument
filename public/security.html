How your data is protected

Straight answers about what Trackument collects, where it lives, and who can see it.

Employee names never leave the browser

Real names, dates, and other identifying details are replaced with placeholders on your device before anything is sent. Trackument's servers and the AI model that drafts your document never see a real name.

What data does Trackument collect?

The facts you enter about an incident: what happened, when, and the employee's position and classification. Names and other identifying details are replaced with placeholders before they ever leave your browser.

Does anything train the AI model?

No. Nothing your district enters is used to train any AI model. Your data is used only to generate your documentation, and for nothing else.

Where is data stored?

Trackument runs on Railway, hosted in the United States. Anything stored in the database is encrypted at rest.

How is data encrypted?

All traffic between your browser and Trackument is encrypted in transit over HTTPS. Data at rest is encrypted by default through Railway.

Who can access what?

API keys and credentials are stored as environment variables, never in the codebase. The codebase lives in a private repository. Application access is currently limited by a password gate during beta, with broader role-based access controls planned as Trackument grows.

How long is data retained?

Trackument does not store generated documents after your session ends. Once you download a document, it's yours to keep in your district's own systems, not in a third-party archive.

Which vendors have access to any data?

A small, disclosed list, each limited to what its specific function needs:

  • Railway: hosting and database
  • Stripe: payment processing
  • Resend: account emails
  • Google: sign-in
  • Anthropic: AI drafting

No analytics vendors, ad networks, or data brokers.

What happens when a district terminates service?

Your district's stored settings and account data are deleted from Trackument's systems. Since generated documents are never retained after a session, there is nothing further to delete there. Contact help@trackument.com to confirm your specific offboarding timeline.
